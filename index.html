    Studio

    About

    A Métis-founded indie studio in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. The founder is Secretary of the Board at SaskInteractive.

    Illicit builds identity, community, and the developer tools that tie independent worlds together. The public Steam profile puts it plainly: former staff at Overwolf and Ex Corp, founder of Illicit Corporation, and a contributor on Klaus Veen's Treason.

    Philosophy

    The surface is underground on purpose. Short names, sharp marks, a hacky streak. Under that is ordinary engineering: accounts, wallets, inventories, and game servers on one spine. Omerta Chat is the web layer, a terminal into the mainframe. From it you can see balance, connections, and the data Mushrüm collects. The same sign-in opens Cod City and gKnuckle.

    What we run

    Omerta Chat is the social hub: chat, a shared economy, and a full arcade. The arcade includes Riff Rush, Pool, Place, and Chess, plus the casino tables Roulette, Crash, and Pachinko. It plays with a keyboard, a gamepad, a guitar, or a Wii Remote. Atlantis Chat is the all-ages sister, the same arcade, collection, and chat with a PG feel. It is live at ill.up.railway.app, and the Tumblr at mobs2r.tumblr.com carries the same name.

    Cod City is an unofficial browser FPS built on Wii-era asset research, with shared progression, local split-screen, and items that land in the Omerta locker. gKnuckle is a rogue-lite climbing mod for Garry's Mod. Its portal runs a server browser, a WiKi, the roach MiLL, and WiSHES, where players vote on the odds with COiNS.

    Illicit API is the self-hostable account, wallet, and economy spine behind Omerta, Atlantis, and Вандам(e)баш. Mushrüm is the analytics platform on that API. Illicit AC is the non-invasive anti-cheat those analytics improve. Faked ID is the shared identity. Faked CC is the private way to spend, and the face of operator billing. Resupply Locker carries the same ID into third-party games, including DarkRP and FiveM, and hosts prepaid server access.

    The public Workshop addons are WEBFISHING Name Tags, Junk Jet in Tool Gun, and Neon Error Sign, with 724 active subscribers between them.
