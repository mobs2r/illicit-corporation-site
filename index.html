      About

      Illicit Corporation is a Métis founded independent research & development lab located in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, Canada. The focus of our research & development is game design, specifically with networking or peripherals. The company was founded by Alyx (mobs2r) who is deeply passionate about the foundational values of the lab. We are constantly evolving our team & commitment to mirror these responsibilities.
